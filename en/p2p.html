How it works

One deal step by step

The crypto does not go to the buyer until the seller has received the money, and the seller does not lose the crypto until they confirm the payment.

Sellerhas USDT
Escrow Nezeriyaholds the crypto
Buyerpays in hryvnia
₴
  1. OrderThe buyer picks an ad and creates an order from 50 ₴. The rate is locked at the moment of creation.
  2. EscrowThe seller's crypto is immediately locked in Nezeriya escrow until the order is completed.
  3. PaymentThe buyer transfers hryvnias from their own card of the same bank, taps «I have paid» and adds a receipt.
  4. CheckThe seller checks the incoming payment and confirms the release with a PIN. They have as much time to check as there was to pay.
  5. CreditingEscrow releases the crypto, and it instantly appears in the buyer's Nezeriya Wallet.

Deal security

Protection of every deal

Escrow

The seller's crypto is locked for the whole order, and nobody can take it around the rules.

Release via PIN

Before the release, the seller answers «Did you really receive it?» and enters a PIN.

Trader check mark

It goes to merchants with turnover from 10 000 USDT, an account older than 100 days and 500+ successful orders.

Order chat

Communication only in the order chat. Phone numbers and links are hidden automatically.

Arbitration

In a dispute the order is frozen, and the arbiter decides within 24 hours. Fraud is punished with a deposit fine and a ban.

Timers

An order not paid in time is cancelled automatically, and if the seller does not check the payment in time, a dispute opens.

P2P questions

What is Nezeriya P2P?

It is a section of the wallet where users buy and sell USDT and GRAM for hryvnias directly from merchants. Nezeriya holds the seller's crypto in escrow until the deal is completed.

How much does a deal cost me?

There is no fee for the buyer and seller on an ad. Merchants pay 0.05% of turnover.

Which card can I pay from?

Only from your own card of the same bank as the seller's (for example, monobank to monobank). The payer's full name must match the name in your P2P account. Otherwise the arbiter returns the crypto to the seller.

What happens if I don't pay in time?

The order is cancelled automatically. The buyer can cancel an order manually only before tapping «I have paid».

And if the seller doesn't confirm the payment?

The seller has as much time to check as there was to pay. If they miss it, a dispute opens automatically, and the crypto stays in escrow.

The bank blocked my card during a deal. What should I do?

Don't cancel the order and don't transfer again. Open a dispute and attach the bank's letter: the crypto stays in escrow until the arbiter decides.

How are disputes resolved?

During a dispute the order is frozen. The arbiter decides within 24 hours based on the chat, receipts and the action log. Fraud is punished with a deposit fine and a ban.

How do I become a merchant?

Make a 10 USDT security deposit on the TON network, add your payment details and create an ad in 3 steps. The deposit is returned automatically after 60 days.

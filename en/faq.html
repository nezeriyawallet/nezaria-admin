Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Didn't find an answer? Write to support and we will reply on Telegram.

General

What is Nezeriya Wallet?

It is a crypto wallet that works in Telegram as a mini app. Open the official app @NezeriyaWalletApp, create a wallet, and you can store, send and exchange crypto.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The wallet opens right in Telegram on your phone or computer.

Which languages are supported?

The wallet is available in more than 200 languages, including Ukrainian, English, Polish and many others.

What are the official Nezeriya accounts?

App: @NezeriyaWalletApp. Bot: @Nezeriya_Wallet_Bot. Channel: @NezeriyaWallet. Support: @Nezeriya_Support. Any other accounts are not official.

Wallet

How do I top up the wallet?

Open “Receive”, choose the asset and network, then copy the address or show the QR code. Top-ups are free.

Can I store NFTs?

Yes. The WALLET account has a separate NFT tab showing all your collections, and any NFT can be sent. Next to it is the MEME tab for meme tokens, and the BANK account holds stocks and metals.

How much does a transfer cost?

A TON transfer costs a 0.1 USDT service fee plus the network fee. We charge no fee of our own for BNB Smart Chain transfers, you pay only network gas. All fees are on the Fees page.

What is NZR?

Nezeriya bonus points. They are earned in games, through the referral program and achievements, and can be exchanged or withdrawn as Telegram Stars.

I forgot my PIN. What should I do?

Write to support @Nezeriya_Support. Never send your secret phrase to anyone, not even support.

Nezeriya Card

What is the difference between CARD CIS and CARD EUROPE?

CARD CIS is made for shopping in every country: online, with Apple Pay, Google Pay and other Pays, but transfers between users are not available on it. CARD EUROPE lets you send money card-to-card to other users and withdraw the balance back to the wallet many times faster.

Is verification required?

No, you don't need documents to open a card. A name in Latin letters and an email are enough.

How much does the card cost?

CARD CIS costs $15 one-time, CARD EUROPE costs $20 one-time, and maintenance is free. The first 50 CIS cards sell for $10, and CIS also costs $10 if you have invited 10 friends.

Can I add the card to Apple Pay?

Yes. You can add the card to Apple Pay or Google Pay manually, and the wallet shows the confirmation code.

What happens to the balance if I close the card?

The balance goes back to the wallet, and Nezeriya charges no fee for it.

Data and security

What data about me is stored?

The full list with explanations is on the What data the bot collects page.

How do I delete my data?

Write to @Nezeriya_Support asking to delete your account. What exactly is deleted is described in the Privacy policy.

Who sees my messages to the bot?

We do not store messages to the bot in the database, but they briefly end up in technical server logs. Support chats are stored so operators can help you.

Features

What you can do with the card

Purchases

Pay for online services, subscriptions and in-store purchases wherever VISA is accepted.

Contactless payment

Add the card to Apple Pay or Google Pay. The wallet shows the confirmation code.

Card details in the wallet

The number, CVV and expiry date can be viewed in the wallet whenever you need them.

Instant notifications

The bot notifies you about every purchase, refund and top-up.

Freeze

Temporarily freeze the card with one tap and unfreeze it when needed.

Transaction history

All card purchases and top-ups are visible in the wallet.

How to open

Four steps

Terms

In the wallet, open the “Card” tab and accept the terms and fees.

Name

Enter your first and last name in Latin letters. No documents needed.

Contacts

Email is needed to register the card, phone to contact you.

Payment

Pay for issuance in USDT. For CARD EUROPE the TRC20, BEP20, ERC20 and Polygon networks are available. The card is usually ready in a few minutes.

Open a Nezeriya Card

The card is opened in the wallet, in the “Card” tab.

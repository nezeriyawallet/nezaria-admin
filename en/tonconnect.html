Protocol

What is TON Connect

TON Connect is the open standard of the TON network that wallets use to talk to apps. The app sends a request, the wallet shows it to you, and only after you confirm does the transaction go to the network.

  • One wallet for all of TON: no need to create new accounts or move funds around.
  • Encrypted channel: requests travel through the TON Connect bridge encrypted, and the bridge cannot read them.
  • You always decide: the connection and every transaction wait for your tap in Nezeriya.

Start

How to connect

Open the app

In a TON app, tap “Connect Wallet”.

Choose Nezeriya

Scan the TON Connect QR code with the scanner on the Nezeriya Home screen, or pick Nezeriya Wallet in the wallet list if the app shows it.

Check and connect

Nezeriya shows the app's name and website. If everything is right, tap “Connect”.

Confirm transactions

For every request the bot sends you a notification. Check the amount and the recipient and tap “Confirm”.

Features

What you can do with TON Connect

Swaps on DEXes

Swap TON and jettons on TON decentralized exchanges straight from your wallet.

NFT marketplaces

Buy and sell NFTs, and the collections you buy appear in the wallet's NFT tab.

Games and mini apps

Connect the wallet to games and apps in Telegram that accept TON.

DeFi services

Liquidity pools, staking and other TON services work with Nezeriya through TON Connect.

Websites in the browser

TON Connect works beyond Telegram: connect Nezeriya to websites opened in the browser too.

Sign in with your wallet

Some websites ask you to prove the wallet is yours. Nezeriya signs, and no funds move.

Security

Your funds, under your control

Connecting does not give the app access to your money. It can only ask, and you decide.

  • Full details before you sign: recipient and amount of every message, the total, and a flag if it is a contract call.
  • A request lives 5 minutes: if you don't answer, it is declined automatically.
  • Disconnect in one tap: Settings → TON Connect shows every connected app.
  • Be careful with unknown sites: only confirm transactions from apps you trust.

FAQ

TON Connect questions

Can an app withdraw my funds?

No. Once connected, the app only sees your TON address. You see and confirm every transaction in Nezeriya, and nothing is sent without it.

Where is the QR scanner?

The wallet's Home screen has a scanner button. Point the camera at a TON Connect QR code and Nezeriya opens the connection request.

How do I disconnect an app?

Open Settings → TON Connect. It lists your connected apps with a “Disconnect” button next to each one.

How much do TON Connect transactions cost?

Nezeriya charges no service fee for TON Connect transactions. You only pay the TON network fee.

What if I don't answer a request?

A transaction request is valid for 5 minutes. If you don't confirm it, it is declined automatically and the app receives a refusal.

How will I know about a new request?

The Nezeriya bot sends a notification with an “Open request” button. It opens the wallet right on the confirmation screen.

Transparency

What data the bot collects

We checked the bot's code, server and connected services. Below is everything the bot stores and who it shares it with. As of 10 November 2026.

Messages

Messages to the bot are not stored in the database but do end up in technical logs.

Telegram ID

Recorded. Without it the account cannot be linked to a wallet.

Name and contacts

Name and username are stored; phone and email only if you provided them.

Other services

Some data is passed to the card issuer and blockchain services (see below).

Who has access

Owner

Has access to the servers, database and control panel (Render, Supabase).

Support

Operators see only their own chats with customers and the data needed to help.

No one else

We do not sell data or share it with ad networks.

Detailed rules, retention periods and the deletion procedure are described in the Privacy policy.

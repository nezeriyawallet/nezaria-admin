Features

What NEZI can do

By voice

Tap the microphone and speak. The text appears live, even a quiet voice is recognised, and you can correct it before sending.

Chat history

All conversations are saved in a searchable history, so it's easy to get back to the answer you need.

Phone and computer

On a computer you get a chat list on the left and quick questions on the home screen, and Ctrl/Cmd+K opens a new chat.

Learns from ratings

Mark answers as helpful or not. NEZI reuses the helpful ones, and the team reviews and fixes the weak ones.

Instant answers

For popular questions about top-ups, withdrawals, cards, PIN, NZR and checks, NEZI answers at once from the team's answer base.

Live rates

NEZI takes crypto and stock prices, exchange rates, fees and card prices straight from the wallet at the moment you ask.

Security

NEZI never asks for your seed phrase, PIN, private key or card codes, and reminds you that nobody from Nezeriya ever does.

People nearby

If your question is about a specific transaction, NEZI will point you to live support at @Nezeriya_Support.

Personality

A little black bull in a sweater

NEZI has a personality of its own. It greets you, listens, thinks and types along with you, and its mood changes during the conversation.

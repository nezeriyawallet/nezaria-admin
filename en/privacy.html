Privacy policy

How Nezeriya collects, uses and protects your data in the @Nezeriya_Wallet_Bot bot, the @NezeriyaWalletApp app, Nezeriya Wallet and Nezeriya Card. Version of 10 November 2026.

ContentsWhat data is collectedWhat it is used forWhere it is storedWho has accessHow long it is keptHow to delete dataYour rightsPolicy changesAdministrator contacts

This policy explains what data is processed by the @Nezeriya_Wallet_Bot bot, the official app @NezeriyaWalletApp and related Nezeriya services (hereinafter “Nezeriya”, “we”). It is based on a review of the bot's code, its server and connected services. By using the bot, you agree to it.

1. What data is collected

Data from Telegram (automatic)

  • Telegram ID is stored because it identifies your account.
  • Username and name from your Telegram profile.
  • Profile photo (a cached copy for display).
  • Interface language and time zone.

Data you provide yourself

  • Phone number, if you tapped “Share contact”. We accept only your own number and use it to detect your country.
  • Email, first and last name in Latin letters when you open a Nezeriya Card.
  • PIN: we store only its bcrypt hash, never the PIN itself.
  • Support messages, chat rating and feedback.

Wallet and transaction data

  • Wallet addresses (TON, BSC, TRON) and public keys.
  • Private keys in encrypted form (AES-256-GCM). The Nezeriya wallet is custodial: keys are stored on our server to sign your transactions.
  • Transaction history: amounts, addresses, transaction hashes, exchanges, games, NZR points, referrals and friends list.
  • Card data: status, last 4 digits, expiry date, number of payments. We do not store the full card number or CVV.
  • Sessions: when the mini app was opened and last active.

What we do not store

  • The text of messages to the bot is not written to the database. It does, however, end up in technical server logs (event log), which are kept for a limited time.
  • We do not store IP addresses in the database. It is used in server memory to protect against overload and may appear in hosting logs.
  • We do not collect documents, ID cards or face photos of customers.

The full data table is on the What data the bot collects page.

2. What it is used for

  • Create and manage the wallet, and carry out transfers, exchanges and payments.
  • Issue and service Nezeriya Card.
  • Send notifications about top-ups, card payments, bonuses and games (they can be turned off).
  • Protect against fraud: verify sign-ins, limit PIN attempts.
  • Provide support.
  • Keep anonymised statistics (number of users, online, turnover) to develop the product.
  • Credit referral bonuses, ranks and achievements.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with ad networks.

3. Where it is stored

  • The main database (PostgreSQL) and the bot server are hosted on Render in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Support chats are stored in the Supabase database.
  • Card data (email, name on card, transactions) is also kept by the card issuer cryptoinfra, which processes it under its own rules.
  • Blockchain transactions (TON, BNB Smart Chain, TRON) are public and stored in the networks themselves.

4. Who has access

  • The owner of Nezeriya has access to the servers, database and control panel.
  • Support operators see their own chats with customers and the customer's name and username in the panel. Access is granted by the owner and can be revoked at any time.
  • Processors in the table above receive only the data they need to do their job.
  • We may disclose data upon a lawful request from public authorities.

5. How long it is kept

  • Account and wallet data is kept while you have an account. Inactive accounts are not deleted automatically.
  • Transaction history is kept while the account exists and may be kept longer if the law or a dispute requires it.
  • Support chats are kept while the account exists or until you request deletion.
  • Technical server logs (including the text of messages to the bot) are kept for a limited time according to Render hosting settings and deleted automatically.
  • Blockchain records cannot be deleted.

6. How to request deletion

Self-service account deletion is not yet available in the wallet, so the request has to be sent manually:

  • Write to @Nezeriya_Support from the Telegram account whose data should be deleted and say “Delete my data”.
  • Before deletion, withdraw all funds from the wallet and card. Once the keys are deleted, access to the funds will be impossible.
  • We will delete or anonymise your data within 30 days and let you know.
  • We may keep only what the law requires (for example, records of financial transactions) and what cannot be deleted technically (blockchain records).
  • Data held by the card issuer cryptoinfra is deleted under its own rules, and we will pass your request on to it.

7. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correction of inaccuracies, restriction of processing or deletion. Notifications can be turned off in the wallet settings. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can contact the data protection authority of your country.

8. Policy changes

We update this policy when the bot or connected services change. The new version is published on this page with a date, and we will announce significant changes in the @NezeriyaWallet channel.

9. Administrator contacts

  • Support and data requests: @Nezeriya_Support
  • Administrator and responsible person: the owner of Nezeriya @FounderTemshchik
  • Official channel: @NezeriyaWallet

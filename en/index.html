Meet NEZI

NEZI is Nezeriya's own AI agent that lives right inside the wallet. It shows you how to do anything in Nezeriya, quotes live rates and fees, and explains finance in plain words.

  • Text or voice: speak, and the text appears live.
  • Live data: crypto, stock and currency rates, fees and card prices.
  • Action buttons: Top up, Send, Exchange right under the answer.

New · TON Connect

One wallet for all of TON

Nezeriya supports TON Connect: connect your wallet to exchanges, NFT marketplaces and games on TON and confirm every transaction in Telegram.

  • Scanner on the Home screen: scan a TON Connect QR code and the request opens in the wallet.
  • Only your address: the app cannot move funds without your confirmation.
  • No service fee: TON Connect transactions only cost the TON network fee.

Nezeriya Card

Two cards. CIS and EUROPE.

A virtual VISA card you can open right in the wallet. Top it up with USDT and pay online, in stores and with Apple Pay or Google Pay.

  • No KYC: no documents needed, the card is issued in minutes.
  • Free maintenance, only a one-time issuance fee.
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay: add the card to your phone.

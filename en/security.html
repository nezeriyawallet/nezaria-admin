Security

Your funds are protected

What we do to protect the wallet, keys and transactions.

Key encryption

Wallet keys are stored on the server only in encrypted form (AES-256-GCM) and decrypted for a moment when you sign a transaction.

PIN

We do not store your PIN, only its bcrypt hash. After several failed attempts, access is temporarily blocked.

Sign-in via Telegram

Every mini app request is verified with Telegram's signature, so nobody can open your wallet from another account.

Bot protection

The number of server requests is limited so bots cannot brute-force PINs or overload the service.

Card data

We do not store the card number or CVV. The card issuer shows them on request, and our database keeps only the last 4 digits.

Secure infrastructure

Servers and the database are located in a data center in Frankfurt (EU), and connections are encrypted with HTTPS.

Important. Nezeriya staff will never ask for your PIN, secret phrase or SMS code. Support works only through @Nezeriya_Support, and any other account claiming to be us is a scam.

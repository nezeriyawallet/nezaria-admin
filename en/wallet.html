How it looks

WALLET and BANK accounts

The home screen is made of account cards that swipe sideways. WALLET holds crypto, NFTs and meme tokens, the “Send”, “Top up” and “Exchange” buttons and history. BANK holds stocks (STOCKS) and metals (METALS). The wallet works on phone and computer.

  • Creating a wallet takes less than a minute and is protected by a PIN.
  • Import an existing wallet with its secret phrase.
  • Transaction history shows every transaction with a blockchain link.

Features

What the wallet can do

Asset exchange

Exchange TON, USDT, gold and tokenized stocks via STON.fi and DeDust, and BNB, USDT BEP20 and USDC via PancakeSwap. The rate is calculated before you confirm.

Transfers

Send crypto to any address or to another Nezeriya user by their ID. The network fee is shown before sending.

QR codes

Every wallet has its own QR code. Show it to receive a transfer, or scan someone else's to send.

Radar

Shake your phone and the wallet shows Nezeriya users nearby you can send money to right away.

Checks and invoices

Create checks and invoices right in the bot chat. One multi-check can be activated by up to 100 people.

Telegram Stars

Buy Stars and withdraw them via NZR points.

Metals

The BANK account has a METALS tab: keep part of your funds in gold (XAUt0 token, Tether Gold).

Stocks

BANK account, STOCKS tab: tokenized shares of Apple, Tesla, Google, Microsoft, Amazon and Nvidia that you can buy with USDT or GRAM.

NFT

Keep NFTs in the wallet: a separate tab shows all your collections, and any NFT can be sent to another user.

Meme tokens

A separate MEME tab for TON meme tokens such as DOGS and NOT.

Games

Wheel of fortune, Plinko, Double, slots and daily spins where you can win NZR points.

Ranks and achievements

Levels from 0 to 100 with rank names and rewards for wallet activity.

Friends and referrals

Add friends to your contacts, invite them with your referral link and earn NZR.

Notifications

The bot notifies you about top-ups, new NFTs, referral bonuses and new daily spins in games. Any notification can be turned off.

200+ languages and currencies

The interface is available in more than 200 languages, including Ukrainian, English, Polish and others, and your balance can be shown in the fiat currency you prefer.

Nezeriya Card

A VISA card you can open and top up with USDT without leaving the wallet.

How to start

Open the app

Find the official app @NezeriyaWalletApp in Telegram and tap “Start”.

Create a wallet

Tap “Open wallet”, set a PIN and save your secret phrase.

Top up

Copy the address or QR code and send crypto to it.

Use it

Send, exchange, open a card and play for NZR.
